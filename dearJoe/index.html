ABOUT US

Welcome to Dear Joe Ventures, a dynamic company founded by Manuel "Manny" Villar Jr., a visionary Filipino entrepreneur and leader. At Dear Joe, we are dedicated to creating memorable experiences and meaningful connections through our diverse range of ventures across real estate, retail, and hospitality sectors.

From iconic coffee shops to vibrant restaurants and retail outlets, Dear Joe Ventures is synonymous with creativity, reliability, and integrity. We take pride in our heritage while embracing the future with a spirit of innovation and adaptability.

Join us on our journey as we strive to make a positive impact on society, one venture at a time. Experience the essence of Dear Joe Ventures – where every moment is crafted with care, and every interaction leaves a lasting impression.
